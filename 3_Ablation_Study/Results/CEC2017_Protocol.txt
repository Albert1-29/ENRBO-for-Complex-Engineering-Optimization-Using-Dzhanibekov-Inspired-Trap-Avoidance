CEC2017: F1-F30 except F2; dimensions 10/30/50/100; bounds [-100,100].
Raw objectives drive optimization. Statistics use f(x)-f*. Errors below 1e-8 are recorded as zero.
Convergence: across-run arithmetic mean error on a logarithmic axis; zero is displayed at the labeled floor.
Left surface: raw objective slice with all coordinates after x2 fixed to zero. Box plots: final-run errors.
Sheet1: basic error statistics. Sheet2: original per-function Friedman mean ranks across run rows, Average and Rank.
Sheet3: original unadjusted two-sided rank-sum p-values and signs using mean-error direction.
Sheet4: two-sided rank-sum p-values corrected by Holm over all functions/opponents WITHIN EACH dimension.
Sheet5: rank effects P(target<opponent)-P(target>opponent).
Sheet6: across-run mean-error ranks and overall Friedman test with functions as blocks. Radar uses these same ranks.
Sheet2 retains the original descriptive run-row ranks; use the function-block test in Sheet6 for overall inference.
Win/loss require adjusted p<0.05 and a positive/negative rank effect. Tie means no detected significant difference, not equivalence.
Overall win/tie/loss counts describe the sum across dimensions; they are not an additional global significance test.
The horizontal radar panels follow the dimension order in the experiment manifest.
